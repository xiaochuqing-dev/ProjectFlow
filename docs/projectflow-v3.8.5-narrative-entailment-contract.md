# V3.8.5 Narrative Entailment Contract

This contract controls the public wording of every automatically generated Story and Chapter. It is Provider-neutral and does not change ProjectFact, raw events, Technical Atoms, membership, chronology or Evidence.

The engineering layer classifies each Story as exactly one of PLANNED, DECLARED, CONFIGURED, IMPLEMENTED, OBSERVED, VERIFIED, REMOVED, RESTORED, UNKNOWN or CONFLICTED. Classification consumes Technical Atoms without flattening their subject/action relationship. It supplies the only allowed subject label, action, outcome, direct support, indirect context, allowed claims, forbidden claims and eligible reason Evidence. The model may paraphrase within that envelope; it cannot strengthen the state.

PLANNED cannot become implemented. DECLARED cannot become verified. CONFIGURED cannot become deployed. IMPLEMENTED without direct independent validation Evidence cannot become stable, production-ready or verified. OBSERVED cannot become implemented or verified. UNKNOWN and CONFLICTED cannot become a positive strong fact. Negative wording such as “不能确认已经实现” is not treated as a positive implementation claim.

Same-commit membership, temporal proximity, shared affected area, subject similarity and Supporting Story links are indirect context only. They never promote another subject. A strong claim must be supported by a direct Atom whose stable subject, Evidence class and action match that claim. Supporting Evidence cannot raise its Primary Story.

An internal `project-area-*` key is an engineering region, not a precise feature subject. Even when that region contains implementation-class files, its first-layer Claim is capped at OBSERVED: the files prove that the area changed, but not that an unspecified feature was implemented or verified. A precise subject with directly matching implementation Evidence keeps normal IMPLEMENTED or VERIFIED eligibility.

A non-empty reason requires at least one reasonEvidenceRef already allow-listed for the Story. Commit messages, file names, model agreement and README language are not sufficient on their own. Every model output template starts with one natural sentence explaining that the reason cannot yet be confirmed. The model may clear it only when both reason and reasonEvidenceRefs are non-empty; merely having optional eligible Evidence is not enough.

Validation rejects unsupported objects, raw paths or filenames, internal enum/token leakage, fixture identifiers, state upgrades, unsupported reasons, repeated Title/Summary/Before/Change/After wording, and vague or list-like Chapter synthesis. The Story title/summary pair must also state an action, understandable object and explicit supported result. If otherwise safe Provider wording omits that result, ProjectFlow retains the already validated deterministic title/summary, marks the Story `MODEL_VALIDATED_WITH_DETERMINISTIC_TITLE`, and increments `modelDeterministicTitleFallbackCount`; it does not silently present the weaker model pair as fully model-authored. One Provider-neutral regeneration is allowed from the original bounded input and must use the matching Story or Chapter schema; rejected raw output is never repeated across either stage. A second failure keeps the deterministic narrative and exposes degraded diagnostics. Both generated and deterministic wording pass the same gate.
