# V3.8.5 Chapter Narrative Contract

A Chapter is a phase-level synthesis of its engineering-selected Primary Stories. Supporting work remains reachable through those Primary Stories but is not sent as an independent topic list.

The model may return only chapterId, title and summary. storyRefs, time boundaries, boundary signals, counts, raw-event membership, coverage and presentation revision remain engineering-owned.

The title names the phase outcome or direction. The summary explains how the confirmed primary changes form that phase. It must share supported meaning with the Primary Story wording and must not concatenate subjects, lead with item counts, expose paths or internal identifiers, invent maturity, or turn declarations/configuration into verified production results.

Small and large histories use the same contract. A large Chapter can receive a second bounded synthesis only after all included Primary Story wording has already passed validation. Failure retains the deterministic Chapter and explicit diagnostics.
