# V3.8.5 Chapter Narrative Contract

A Chapter is a phase-level synthesis of its engineering-selected Primary Stories. Supporting work remains reachable through those Primary Stories but is not sent as an independent topic list.

Temporal proximity and same-commit membership are not sufficient to make several independent outcomes one Chapter. When distinct Primary subjects share a generic commit, and every Supporting Story already has an explicit Primary owner, the engineering layer splits the temporal group into one Chapter per owned outcome and records `INDEPENDENT_OUTCOME_BOUNDARY`. If ownership is incomplete it keeps the original group rather than guessing. The model never performs this split and cannot change the resulting membership.

The model may return only chapterId, title and summary. storyRefs, time boundaries, boundary signals, counts, raw-event membership, coverage and presentation revision remain engineering-owned.

The title names at least one concrete phase outcome. The summary explains how the confirmed Primary changes form that phase and must let a reader repeat what was actually completed. Merely saying that work was “围绕某主题推进”“逐步形成”“得到完善” or “完成相关建设” is insufficient even when a broad theme overlaps. It must share a concrete supported action/object/result with Primary Story wording and must not concatenate subjects, lead with item counts, expose paths or internal identifiers, invent maturity, or turn declarations/configuration into verified production results.

Small and large histories use the same contract. Chapter boundaries remain deterministic, chronology- and Evidence-driven; RC3 may apply the explicit independent-outcome rule above but cannot let a model silently change membership or lose events. A large Chapter can receive a second bounded synthesis only after all included Primary Story wording has already passed validation. Chapter prompt v6 uses its own one-time repair contract: it regenerates only from the original CHAPTER_SYNTHESIS_JSON and can return only chapterId, title and summary; it must never be redirected to the Story-only OUTPUT_TEMPLATE_JSON protocol. Failure retains a concrete deterministic Chapter and explicit diagnostics.
